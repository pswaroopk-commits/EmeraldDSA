FAQ

Common Loan Questions

These answers help with the first conversation. Final eligibility, rates and approval are always decided by the lender.

Which loan services does Emerald DSA support?

Home, car, mortgage, business, personal, Mudra, top-up, construction and loan-against-property enquiries.

Can I apply by phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. You can call or send a WhatsApp enquiry, then the team will guide you through the next documents and lender process.

Does Emerald DSA approve loans directly?

No. Emerald DSA assists with guidance and coordination. The bank or financial institution decides eligibility, approval and final terms.

What documents are usually discussed first?

Identity proof, address proof, income details, bank statements and property or vehicle details may be requested depending on the loan type.
